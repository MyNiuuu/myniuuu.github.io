Muyao Niu (牛慕尧)

Hi, I'm Muyao Niu. I am a 2nd year Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechano-Informatics, the University of Tokyo (UTokyo). My supervisor is Prof. Yinqiang Zheng.

I received my master's degree from The University of Tokyo in 2024, and B.E. degree from Dalian University of Technology in 2022.

Email Github Google Scholar WeChat: MyNiuuu

I expect to graduate from UTokyo in September 2027 and am looking for full-time opportunities starting in fall 2027. Feel free to reach out via email or WeChat! ☕️

My research interests include:

  • Generative Modeling (video & 3D): I explore video and 3D representations for controllable generation. I believe that hybrid representations are the future of generative modeling.

    Works: MOFA-Video, WorldSculpt, AniCrafter, RASC, CompoSIA, CV-VAE, StereoCrafter, Surprise-Forcing, etc.

  • NeRF & 3DGS: I study 3D reconstruction from various observations by jointly modeling scene structure and imaging process.

    Works: MAD-Avatar, 3DarkFusion, RS-NeRF, Visionary, ToMiE, etc.

  • Computational Photography: I believe that photography-aware modeling will be even more important when AGI comes.

    Works: VCSD, AdvNIR, NVEU, SpaceDG, Event-Attack, etc.

Selected Papers 📃

MOFA-Video: Controllable Image Animation via Generative Motion Field Adaptions in Frozen Image-to-Video Diffusion Model

Muyao Niu, Xiaodong Cun, Xintao Wang, Yong Zhang, Ying Shan, Yinqiang Zheng

ECCV, 2024

project page / paper / code

We introduce MOFA-Video to adapt motions from different domains to the frozen Video Diffusion Model. MOFA-Video can effectively animate a single image using various types of control signals, including trajectories, keypoint sequences, and their combinations.

We leverage "3DGS Avatar + Background Video" as hybrid guidance for the video diffusion model to insert and animate anyone into any scene following given motion sequence.

Visibility Constrained Wide-band Illumination Spectrum Design for Seeing-in-the-Dark

Muyao Niu, Zhuoxiao Li, Zhihang Zhong, Yinqiang Zheng

CVPR, 2023

paper / code

We designed an optimal illumination spectrum in the VIS-NIR range by considering human vision constraints, which significantly improves translation performance. A fully differentiable model was proposed, which includes the imaging process, human visual perception, and the enhancement network.

Physics-Based Adversarial Attack on Near-Infrared Human Detector for Nighttime Surveillance Camera Systems

ACM MM, 2023

paper / code

NIR-assisted Video Enhancement via Unpaired 24-hour Data

Muyao Niu, Zhihang Zhong, Yinqiang Zheng

ICCV, 2023

paper / code

We addressed the issue of collecting data for utilizing NIR images to improve low-light VIS videos. Physics-inspired algorithms are designed to simulate pseudo paired data of NIR and VIS images, simulating day-to-night situations. We then trained an enhancement network using the generated pseudo data.
